# OTT Platforms Merger Analysis

[📊 View Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/farook.ahmed.ali.shaik/viz/RPC14_17487526849010/Home?publish=yes)

In this project, I analyzed the merger of **LioCinema** and **Jotstar** (OTT Platforms) to uncover key trends in the fast-changing OTT industry. The analysis focused on user growth & behaviour patterns, engagement, content consumption and platform performance, using data to support better business decisions.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

**Liocinema**, a telecom giant, is merging with **Jotstar**, a leading streaming platform, to leverage subscriber growth and content diversity. The objective is to optimize business strategies with valuable insights and establish a dominant OTT presence after the merge.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Track user trends, active vs. inactive users, and demographics  
- Analyze content library distribution and watch time patterns  
- Evaluate subscription upgrade, downgrade, and churn behaviors  
- Language diversity, content type preferences  
- Watch time trends by device and city tiers  

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Tableau**: Data Visualisation and Insights  
- **MySQL**: Ad-hoc analysis  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

- **Overview**: Key business metrics at a glance  
- **User Trends**: Growth, engagement insights  
- **Content Library**: Content availability by type and language  
- **Engagement Analysis**: Viewing behavior by device and region  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Here are some highlights from my findings:

- **Subscriber Growth**: LioCinema grew 5x in 2024 but struggles with inactivity (45%). Jotstar has a smaller but highly active paid user base (85% active).  
- **Content Diversity**: Jotstar excels in English/Hindi content for urban audiences, while LioCinema caters to regional languages like Tamil and Telugu.  
- **Revenue Trends**: Jotstar generates 2x more revenue than LioCinema but lacks growth momentum.  

---

## 💡 Impactful Takeaways

These insights helped identify strategies to:

- Boost engagement for inactive users on LioCinema  
- Leverage Jotstar's monetization strategies for paid plans  
- Optimize content offerings post-merger to cater to diverse audiences
